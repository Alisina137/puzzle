# Pricing Analysis

## Competitor Pricing
- Book Bolt: \.99/month (\.99/month annual)
- Puzzle Fast: Free
- My Word Search: Free/Paid features

## Recommended Pricing Model

### Free Tier
- 10 puzzles per book
- 1 book limit
- Basic templates
- Preview only
- Watermarked PDF

### Creator — \-15/month
- 100 puzzles per book
- 5 books
- Individual regeneration
- PDF export
- Standard templates

### Publisher — \-29/month
- Unlimited puzzles
- Unlimited books
- Batch generation
- Advanced templates
- KDP export
- Book series
- AI features

## Pricing Principle
Avoid making the basic product unnecessarily complicated. Users should be able to experience the complete workflow before paying. A limited free book/sample is preferable to hiding the main product experience.
