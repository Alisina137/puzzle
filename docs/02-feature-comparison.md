## Key Conclusion

**Individual regeneration is not completely unique.** PuzzleFast already supports repeatedly remaking a puzzle. However, our product can differentiate by making regeneration a **first-class book-management feature** rather than simply a puzzle-generation button.

Our stronger combination is:
> Generate an entire book → validate everything → regenerate any individual puzzle → drag/reorder puzzles → automatically renumber → automatically synchronize solutions → export.
