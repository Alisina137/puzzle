# Market Trends Analysis

## Publishing Trends
- Puzzle books remain an active KDP category
- Competition is high, but growing
- Higher-quality, differentiated puzzle concepts have a better opportunity
- Generic, mass-produced puzzle books are increasingly crowded

## Target Segments
- Adults
- Seniors / large-print users
- Children
- Teachers
- Hobbyists
- KDP publishers
- Niche/audience-specific puzzle publishers

## Popular Themes
- Animals
- Space
- Travel
- Food
- Sports
- Nature
- History
- Business
- Holidays
- Gardening
- Education

## Important Note
KDP explicitly classifies puzzle books as generally **not low-content books**, which is important for our publishing workflow.

KDP also states that puzzle books are generally better suited to physical formats than Kindle.
